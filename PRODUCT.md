# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are recruiters and prospective clients evaluating Agung J for software engineering and IAM-focused work. They scan quickly, compare multiple candidates, and need confidence signals fast. Their job is to validate capability, execution quality, and professional fit within minutes.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to convert high-intent visitors into direct outreach and deeper technical evaluation. Success means visitors do at least one of two actions after a short pass: send a DM or open GitHub for proof of work. The site should reduce uncertainty quickly, establish trust, and make next steps obvious.

## Brand Personality

Credible, cinematic, precise.

Voice and tone should feel senior, calm, and deliberate. Visual storytelling can be dramatic, but never noisy or performative.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-template aesthetics that feel interchangeable.
- Loud, decorative gradients used without semantic purpose.
- Gimmicky motion that prioritizes spectacle over clarity.
- Bloated sections that restate claims without proof.

## Design Principles

1. Show proof, not claims.
2. Cinematic restraint over visual noise.
3. Frictionless path to DM and GitHub.
4. Trust first: signals of rigor, security, and execution quality.
5. Every section earns its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Ensure clear keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion support for animation-heavy sections. Content hierarchy should remain legible and understandable under zoom and smaller viewports.
